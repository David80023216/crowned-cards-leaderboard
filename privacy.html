Privacy Policy — Crown Hunt

Effective: October 4, 2026 · Crowned Cards

Crown Hunt is a free daily card-pull game run by the Crowned Cards YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the game collects and why.

What we collect

  • Anonymous player ID. When you open the game, Firebase creates a random anonymous ID for your browser so your daily pack and collection can be saved. No name, email, or Google account is required to play.
  • Game activity. Your daily pack pulls (card, rarity, value, date), streaks, referral links you share, and referral rewards you earn. This powers the leaderboard, the live pull ticker, and prize verification.
  • Display handle (optional). If you type a YouTube @handle, it is shown on the leaderboard and in the pull ticker so other players can see who pulled what.
  • Subscription verification (optional). If you choose "Verify my subscription," you sign in with Google and we check once, via the YouTube API, whether you are subscribed to @CrownedCards. We store only the yes/no result and the date — not your watch history, not your other subscriptions.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other websites.
  • We do not collect your real name, address, payment details, or precise location.

Why we collect it

Game data exists to run the game: daily packs, streaks, the leaderboard, referral rewards, cheat detection, and verifying prize winners. Subscription checks exist so prizes go to actual subscribers, as the rules state.

Where data lives

Game data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) under standard Google security. Anonymous IDs live in your own browser; clearing site data removes your local session (your leaderboard entries remain under the anonymous ID).

Your choices

  • Play without a handle — your pulls appear without a name.
  • Skip subscription verification — you can still play; only prize winners must be confirmed subscribers.
  • Ask for deletion: email the address below with your @handle or player ID and we will remove your game records.

Children

Crown Hunt is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please do not play.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
