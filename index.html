📜 HOW TO PLAY

👑 How to play — rip packs, not points — Watch a Crown Hunt video, then comment your pick: name the card that takes the crown. Every correct prediction mints a digital pack pull — a fictional-athlete card with a whole-dollar value. The player with the highest total binder value at month's end is the Top Collector. No points anywhere.

⚔️ Battle predictions — On battle videos, comment your pick before the battle resolves. Correct winner = one standard pull (counts toward the 3/day scoring-pull cap). Picks posted after the winner is declared don't score.

📸 Screenshot claims — In the flash screenshot game, a correct screenshot claim = one standard pull (counts toward the 3/day scoring-pull cap).

🎲 Pull odds (standard pulls) — common $10–50 (60%) · rare $50–250 (25%) · epic $250–1K (10%) · legendary $1K–10K (4%) · mythic $10K–100K (1%).

✨ Every comment gets a card — ANY comment on a hunt video = one fan pull (common-only, $5–25), max 1 per video per Central day. Comment on 3 videos = 3 fan pulls. Nail a pick on that video and your fan pull upgrades to a standard pull. Fan pulls never count toward the scoring cap — just show up and you rip something.

🔥 Streaks = boosted pulls — Play consecutive days (any credited activity keeps the streak alive). Hit 3, 7, 14, or 30 days and you get a streak pull with better odds, once per streak run: 3d — rare 30 / epic 40 / legendary 20 / mythic 10 · 7d — epic 50 / legendary 35 / mythic 15 · 14d — epic 30 / legendary 45 / mythic 25 · 30d — epic 20 / legendary 45 / mythic 35. Miss a day and the streak resets.

🗂️ Your binder — Tap any collector's row on the standings to open their binder: every card they've pulled, with art, name, rarity, value, and how they earned it. Sorts by biggest pulls first.

🏟️ Weekly tournament — Mon–Thu quarterfinals, Fri semifinals, Sat championship, Sun crowning + next week's bracket reveal. Weekly themes rotate. Fair fights only: cards battle within 3x of each other's value. The flash screenshot game runs daily as the side game.

🗓️ Monthly seasons — Binders accumulate all month and reset on the 1st. Season 1 runs Oct 3–31. Nobody's ever too far behind to join.

🏆 Top Collector — The biggest binder value at month's end wins actual physical sports cards, mailed to you (Season 1 pays the top 3: Bird PSA 10 · Wemby Mosaic RC · Jeter Draft Pick). Tiebreaker: most mythics → most legendaries → longest streak. Free entry, 18+, US shipping only. Not sponsored or endorsed by YouTube.

🎮 Digital cards are game pieces only — Crown Hunt cards have no cash value. They can't be redeemed, traded, or sold. The Season 1 prizes are real sports cards awarded by the channel — separate from your digital binder.

⚖️ Fair play — One account per player, no spam-dumping past the daily cap, keep comments clean. The channel's scoring calls are final.
