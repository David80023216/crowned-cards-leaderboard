📖 HOW IT WORKS

The real Season 1 rules

✅ ELIGIBILITYYou must be subscribed to @CrownedCards on YouTube to win. Season winners are verified before prizes ship — if a winner isn't subscribed, they're disqualified and the prize goes to the next top collector.

🎯 Set bonusComplete all 3 cards in a rarity set to bank a bonus equal to the set's total card value, rounded up. The bonus counts toward your collection value — once per set, duplicates don't pay twice.

💬 Comment = cardEvery comment earns an instant card: 70% Common / 22% Rare / 8% Epic — capped at Epic. One card per video per day.

🎯 🏆 Winning pick = better cardCall the battle winner before the crowning and your comment card levels up to full pack odds: 60% / 25% / 10% / 4% / 1% — Legendary and Grail unlocked. No second card; your card gets better.

⏰ Pick windowThe pick window closes when the winner’s crowned — late comments still get the instant card, but can’t level up.

👑 Top Collector winsThe leaderboard ranks by total collection value. Most Grails, then most Legendaries, then longest streak breaks ties.

🔥 Streaks boost oddsComment daily to build a streak — milestone streaks pay boosted-odds pulls with no commons.

🃏 Digital cards = game onlyNo cash value. Season 1 (Oct 3–31) pays real card prizes to the Top Collector.
